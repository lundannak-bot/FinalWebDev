How to Play

  • Fill every row, column, and 3×3 box with the digits 1–9, each appearing exactly once.
  • Click a cell, then tap a number (or use your keyboard 1–9).
  • Toggle Notes to pencil in candidates without committing.
  • Use Hint to reveal one correct cell (3 hints per game).
  • You have 3 mistakes before the game ends.
